Building
Footprint & floors
A and C are L long, B and D are W long. A facing 180° = south; the four edges run A, B, C, D clockwise.
Floor groups
Floors are grouped from the bottom up; the last group takes whatever is left. Each group can set back on four sides or shift as a whole. + adds a group, Del removes the current one.
Windows
One continuous window band per wall segment; its height follows the window-wall ratio.
Environment & calculation
How far from the window counts as the"window-side room band". Overheating and daylight are evaluated in this band; deeper floor area is not counted.
Off = sliders respond faster; the sun-hours and exposure columns keep their last values.
Topology operations
Each operation can be repeated: + adds one, Del removes the current one. The coloured points in Rhino are handles (red notch, blue courtyard, green split); dragging one is the same as moving a slider, the later move wins.
Notch from Rhino
Deeper than half makes a U; the deepest stops 1 m before the far wall. Two notches closer than 1 m: the later one is ignored.
Courtyard from Rhino
The four inner walls of a courtyard get windows facing in. Keep it 1 m from the outer wall and clear of notches and other courtyards, or it is ignored. Opened only in lower groups, the floors above cover it.
Split from Rhino
Cuts the slab in two with a gap between. The walls along the gap are facades too (S1, S2) with windows and devices. No cut if the line crosses a notch or courtyard, or leaves a side under 2 m.

Whole building overheating (bare → with device)— Whole building daylight (bare → with device)— Whole building overheating (no notch → with notch, both bare)— Whole building daylight (no notch → with notch, both bare)— Winter net gain (bare → with device)—
Overheating = share of hours above 25 °C indoors over the year; Passive House allows at most 10 %, 5 % is recommended. Daylight = mean daylight factor of the room band, 3 % counts as sufficient. “bare / with” = without / with the shading device. Exposure = how much direct sun this segment still gets after the building massing itself blocks part of it. Sun = = Ladybug direct sun hours from Ladybug. Overheating and daylight are estimates: good for comparing, not for absolute values.
